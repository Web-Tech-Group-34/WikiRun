Rules

Welcome to the race!

Wiki Speed Run is all about navigating from one Wikipedia article to another—using only internal links—as fast as possible. Think you've got what it takes? Here's how to play:

Objective

Your goal is simple:

Start at a given Wikipedia article and reach the target article as quickly as possible—using only the links found within each page's content. No shortcuts. Just brainpower and speed.

  • Start the Game

    • You’ll be given a start article and a target article. Your timer begins as soon as you see the start page.

  • Click Through Wikipedia

    • Use only blue internal links in the body of the article to move from page to page. Every click matters!

  • Reach the Target

    • Once you land on the target page, the clock stops. The faster you find it, the better your time!

Ready to start Clicking?

Put your skills to the test and see how fast you can go. Every second counts in the world of Wiki Speed Run!
