About Us

Welcome to Wiki Speed Run, an exciting game that challenges your strategy and speed as you race through Wikipedia articles! Whether you're a casual gamer or looking to make learning fun, Wiki Speed Run offers a unique and engaging experience for everyone.

Our Mission

At Wiki Speed Run, our mission is simple: to create a fun, educational platform that combines the excitement of gaming with the endless information found on Wikipedia. Players race against the clock to navigate through Wikipedia's network of articles, using only internal links to reach a designated target page. It's a game of speed, strategy, and quick thinking, all while exploring the fascinating world of Wikipedia!

What makes Wiki Speed Run special?

Focused on blending knowledge with strategy. Our game is more than just fast-paced, it gets you thinking! Players must think critically and make quick decisions, all while navigating through Wikipedia's vast collection of interconnected articles.

Our Commitment to Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to enjoy Wiki Speed Run, regardless of ability or background. That's why we've prioritized accessibility in every aspect of the game: Readable Fonts & clear colours; Clear Contrast: Our design ensures readability, making sure all players can enjoy the game with ease. Simple, Consistent Layout: We've kept things simple and consistent so players can stay focused.
